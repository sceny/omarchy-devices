# Set up calls here

Once per phone. The computer becomes the phone's hands-free, as a car's
is: calls ring here, and their audio comes here when you answer. What it
does: [Calls](../use/calls.md).

On the device's page, **Calls here** does every step it can and says the
one left to you:

1. **Bluetooth** on this computer: turned on, or installed and started (a
   card says what, then your password).
2. **Pair the phone** over Bluetooth, once: on the phone, *Settings ›
   Connected devices › Pair new device*; here, Omarchy's **Bluetooth**
   opens, pick the phone. The row waits, and carries on when it is paired.
3. The phone paired under its own name is used for calls and connected.
   With two phones and neither by that name, rename one on the phone.

Until you answer or call here, the audio stays on the phone: a call you
answer on the phone is not taken over by the computer.

Not wanted? Its switch on the device's page turns it off: calls are then
named here and answered on the phone, and nothing about it is a problem.

## When it stops working

- *Not connected over Bluetooth*: Bluetooth went off on the phone, or it
  is out of reach. *Fix* connects it again; else turn Bluetooth on there.
- *PipeWire's hands-free service is not running*: *Fix* restarts this
  computer's audio.
- A headset on the computer: its microphone needs its own hands-free
  link, so its sound is plainer for the call.
