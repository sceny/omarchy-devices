# Calls

When the phone rings, the bar rings with it, and a card names who is
calling, by the name the phone has for them.

![An incoming call card (demo data)](../images/call-ringing.png)

A missed call stays until you close it, with *Call back*, which dials on
the phone, and *Text back*, which starts a [message](messages.md) to them.

![A missed call card with call back and text back (demo data)](../images/call-missed.png)

Answering here, with the sound, is not there yet.
