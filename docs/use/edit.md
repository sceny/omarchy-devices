# Make it yours

Shape the page on the page itself: press `E`, or right-click it or its
pill. Pick what the pill shows and in what order, which sections show and
in what order, and which shortcuts; the page and the pill change as you
do it. ✓ keeps it; Esc puts it back.

![Editing the page: the bar chip, sections and shortcuts (demo data)](../images/edit.png)

- **The bar strip** leads: the pill as the bar draws it, a little larger;
  *Battery only when low*; *Calls*.
- **Sections** become bars with a grip and a switch: drag to reorder, or
  `Shift+K` / `Shift+J`.
- **Shortcuts** all show: click to add or take away, drag the chosen ones.
- **Per device,** with several; the defaults for all of them are in
  [Settings](../setup/settings.md).

## Keys

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `E` · Esc | Edit, and again to keep · put back |
| `Shift+K` `Shift+J` | Move the section under the cursor |
