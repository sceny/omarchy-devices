# Now playing

The phone's player on your desktop: the cover, the title, seek, skip and
volume, for whatever is playing, just as the phone shows it. The other
players are a swipe or `h` `l` away.

![Now playing: cover, seek bar and volume (demo data)](../images/now-playing.png)

- **The volume** stays where you left it, in the phone's own steps.
- **Folded,** the card keeps its cover and a play button; the
  [bar](bar.md) can show a play mark meanwhile.
- **A player the phone has put away** is gone here too.

## Keys

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Enter · `h` `l` | Play/pause · the other players |
| `[` `]` · `,` `.` · `-` `=` | Skip · seek 10 s · volume |
