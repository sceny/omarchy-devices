# Received

Share a file from the phone, and it is here by the time you look: open
it, show it in Files, or forget it. Rename, move or delete it there, and
the list follows.

![Received: two files with their size and time (demo data)](../images/received.png)

A picture opens as a safe copy, like the [Gallery](gallery.md)'s; any other
file opens in its own app, your choice as in Files.

Setup: [Your devices](../setup/devices.md)
