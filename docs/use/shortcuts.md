# Shortcuts

The things you reach for the phone for, one click each: ring it even on
silent, send it files, your clipboard, a text or a link, open its
[screen](screen.md) or your [messages](messages.md), ping it, play or
pause. Pick which ones show, and their order: [Make it yours](edit.md).

![Shortcuts as tiles (demo data)](../images/shortcuts.png)

What a click came to shows as a toast over the panel, or in Omarchy's own
on-screen display when the panel is closed. Folded, the section is a row
of icons that still work.

## Keys

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `h` `l` · Enter | Along the shortcuts · activate |
